About RMTECH

A technology brand for industrial engineering.

RMTECH is the engineering and technology brand of Ronak Makwana. It brings together electrical engineering, machine automation and industrial digital tools to help manufacturing equipment run more reliably and become easier to monitor.

The work spans control systems, drives and motion, SCADA dashboards and connected monitoring, with a focus on solutions that operators can understand and maintenance teams can support.
