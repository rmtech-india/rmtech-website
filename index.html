A LITTLE ABOUT MY WORK

Where electrical engineering meets intelligent manufacturing.

My work and learning interests sit at the intersection of electrical engineering, industrial automation and digital monitoring. I enjoy understanding how machines operate, improving control strategies and making important operating information easier to access.

From PLC and HMI concepts to SCADA dashboards, energy monitoring and industrial connectivity, I aim to build solutions that are reliable, understandable for operators and useful for decision-makers.

This portfolio is a selected overview of my technical interests and project work. Detailed project information can be added as each case study is approved for public sharing.
